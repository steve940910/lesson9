Poem 1: Chocolate Factory (1)

When I am sad or tired,

I close my eyes and find,

The sweetest place in all the world,

The chocolate factory in my mind!

🏃
Action: Close your eyes. (閉上眼睛)
🗣️
Voice: Sad and tired. (聲音低沉疲倦)

Poem 1: Chocolate Factory (2)

There are rivers of fudge,

And marshmallow land,

Where the magic flows,

Right into my hand!

🏃
Action: Open your hands! (張開雙手迎接魔法)
🗣️
Voice: Happy and Loud! (開心大聲)

Poem 2: The Pizza Debate (1) 🍕

What goes on a pizza?

Let's have a great debate!

Pineapple or not?

What is on your plate?

🤔
Action: Think or shake your head. (做出思考或搖頭的動作)

Poem 2: The Pizza Debate (2) 🍕

"Spicy is best!"

The pepperoni cried,

"Quiet is the quest,"

The mushroom softly sighed.

🗣️
Voice 1: LOUD shout for Spicy! (大聲呼喊)
🤫
Voice 2: Whisper for Quiet. (細聲表達)

Poem 3: Ice Cream Race (1) 🍦

Three scoops race down,

In a summer afternoon!

🖐️
Action: Show 3 fingers! (用手勢比出三個雪糕球)

Poem 3: Ice Cream Race (2) 🍦

Strawberry giggles,

"I will win it soon!"

Chocolate beats with power,

"I am strong and bold!"

Vanilla is calm and wise,

"Just stay nice and cold."

🍓
Strawberry: High & sweet! (高音甜美)
🍫
Chocolate: Strong & confident! (自信有力)
🍦
Vanilla: Calm & gentle. (平靜溫柔)
